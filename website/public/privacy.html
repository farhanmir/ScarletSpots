Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

ScarletSpots provides parking availability and session tracking for Rutgers University students. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how to request access or deletion.

Information We Collect

  • Account data: Rutgers email, profile name, optional avatar.
  • Parking session data: lot ID, start/end timestamps, optional confirmation feedback, and session coordinates when needed for parking-session features.
  • Device and app signals: push token, platform, and app diagnostics for reliability.
  • Location and motion data when permission is granted, including background detection features and parked-car return context.

How We Use Information

  • Show live occupancy and forecasted lot availability.
  • Support friend visibility features inside the Friends tab.
  • Operate auto-park detection, notifications, and offline queue reliability.
  • Improve detection quality and reduce false positives over time.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We use infrastructure providers (Supabase, hosting, and push delivery services) only to run ScarletSpots features.

Your Choices

  • Disable location, motion, or notification permissions in device settings at any time.
  • Request a copy of your account data.
  • Request account deletion and associated data removal.

Data Requests

For data export or deletion requests, email privacy@scarletspots.app.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to privacy@scarletspots.app.
